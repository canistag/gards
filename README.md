# Childcare Cost & Affordability Dashboard
## Description 
A dashboard that visualizes the cost of childcare across all fifty states of the US. 
## Question
- How much money is needed annually to comfortably have a family in each state?
- What are the most and least expensive places to have a family?
## Assumptions
![Family](https://github.com/canistag/childcare/blob/main/family%20visualizer.png)
An assumption I have made is that the average family consists of two parents, two children, a dog, and a cat. In addition to this, each family will have two insured cars, a 3 bedroom 2 bathroom house, and a 401k fund for each parent. For the children, each will have a sport and extracurricular, a Roth IRA savings account, and a 529 college savings account. Each of these funds and or savings accounts will be a recurring expense of $100 each per month ($1200 annually).
